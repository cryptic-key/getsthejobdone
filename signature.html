Email signature

Paste this into Outlook

Copy the signature, then paste it into Outlook’s signature box. The seal, the orange bar, and the links come with it. Paste with Ctrl+V. Paste as plain text drops the design.

What gets copied

Outlook on Windows: File → Options → Mail → Signatures. Click in the editor, then paste.

Save it as your signature

  1. Click Copy signature.
  2. In classic Outlook, open File → Options → Mail → Signatures.
  3. Click New, name it, then click in the edit box.
  4. Press Ctrl+V, then Save.
  5. Choose it for New messages and Replies/forwards.
New Outlook and Outlook on the web
  1. Open Settings → Mail → Compose and reply.
  2. Click in the email signature box and press Ctrl+V.
  3. Turn on the signature for new messages, then save.
Outlook for Mac
  1. Open Outlook → Settings → Signatures.
  2. Click +, click in the editor, and press Command+V.
  3. Set it as the default for new mail and replies.
