Save it in Outlook

  1. Click Copy signature. That copies the dark card only, not the sample email around it.
  2. Classic Outlook: File → Options → Mail → Signatures. Click New, then click in the edit box.
  3. Press Ctrl+V and Save. Choose it for new messages and replies.
  4. New Outlook and Outlook on the web: Settings → Mail → Compose and reply, then paste.
  5. Outlook for Mac: Outlook → Settings → Signatures, then paste with Command+V.

Paste with Ctrl+V. Paste as plain text drops the colors. The typing stays on this page. In the email it reads “Need it to get done”.
